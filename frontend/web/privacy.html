PRIVACY

SaHayak helps people find compatible blood nearby. Developed by Sudeepta Mukherjee. This page is a plain notice, not legal advice.

What we keep

  • Email or mobile to sign in
  • Blood group (health data)
  • Optional phone and location — only when you tap find or send
  • Hospital name and ward you type

Location

  • Only to rank nearby matching people and listed places
  • Never in the background. You may type a city instead

Phone numbers

  • Not on maps
  • Only after someone accepts, and only for that request
  • Family guest links never show a phone

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data
  • Families never pay for Need blood
  • We do not read SMS or place calls from the app

Your control

  • Delete your account from More
  • Data sits on the operator’s machine or later an India-hosted server they control
