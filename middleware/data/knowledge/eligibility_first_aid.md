# Eligibility (informational — not a doctor)

Fasting, fever, or feeling unwell: use self-hold. Do not donate that day.
After whole blood, many programmes wait about 90 days. SaHayak shows a calendar hint only.
Age, weight, and haemoglobin rules belong to the hospital blood bank, not this app.
Never take medical advice from a chat. Follow the doctor and the blood-bank officer.

# First aid (informational — not a substitute for 108 / hospital)

Bleeding: press firmly with a clean cloth. Do not remove the first cloth if it soaks; add another on top. Get to a hospital.
Burns: cool with running water, not ice or toothpaste. Cover loosely. Seek care for large or face burns.
Chest pain / collapse: call emergency services. Do not drive the person yourself if you can get an ambulance.
This is not diagnosis. SaHayak does not treat patients.
